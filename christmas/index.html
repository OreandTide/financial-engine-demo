The two-minute tour

🎄 People — add everyone you're buying for, each with a cap and a line about what they like. Caps are what make December's decisions easy.

💡 Idea Bank — park the maybes with a link and rough price. Promote the keepers to the gift list in one tap.

🎁 Gifts — one card per gift. Move the status as it goes Idea → Bought → Arrived → Wrapped → Given, and type the real price. Mark surprises 🎁 Hidden so a glance over your shoulder gives nothing away.

🏠 Home — what's left, what's safe to spend per gift still owed, who's over cap, and the countdown. Check it December 1 and December 15 and the season behaves.

✧ Ask AI — stuck for an idea, or want a second opinion on the budget? The buttons marked ✧ write a ready-made question from your own list — you read it, then copy it into your own ChatGPT. The app itself sends nothing anywhere.

🦃 Extras, Cards, Deals, Returns — under More: the non-gift money kept in its own lane, the card checklist, Black Friday price-watching, and the returns log.

Where your data lives

Only in this browser, on this device — never uploaded, no account, nothing to log into. That's the whole privacy policy. Back up from Season & Backup now and then, and you're covered if the browser clears its storage.

A planning tool, not financial advice. Buy once, use every year.
