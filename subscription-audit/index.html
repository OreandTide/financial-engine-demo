The 20-Minute Audit

1
Minutes 0–5 — Gather the evidence.
Open your bank and credit card statements from the last two months (three if you can — annual charges hide). Don't judge anything yet.
2
Minutes 5–12 — List everything that repeats.
One entry per recurring charge: the streaming duo, the gym, the storage plan, the app you forgot in March. Bills like internet and insurance count too.
3
Minutes 12–16 — Give every charge a verdict.
Keep means you'd buy it again today at this price. Cancel means it's done. Negotiate means you want it cheaper. Downgrade means the smaller tier would do. Be honest about Last Used — it settles most arguments.
4
Minutes 16–19 — Cancel the easy wins now.
Not this weekend — now, while the list is open. Use “Mark as cancelled” on each one and add the confirmation number. It lands on your Cancellation Log automatically.
5
Minutes 19–20 — Read the Dashboard.
Monthly burn, true yearly cost, and the savings you just found. That's the score. Re-run the audit every few months — subscriptions creep back; that's what they do.

Free trials are the quietest charges of all. Mark them as trials with their end date, and the Dashboard counts down the ones ending in the next 30 days so you decide before they bill — not after.
