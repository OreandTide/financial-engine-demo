Lessons on your route

Short lessons, surfaced because your numbers asked for them.

TRAIL MARKER · DEBT

The Freeze

A minimum payment is designed to shrink — so the debt lingers and the interest piles up. Freeze the payment at today's amount and the same dollars finish years sooner. Nothing about your budget changes; only the ending does.

Applies to you: Card 1 — worth $5,565 and fourteen years.

TRAIL MARKER · SPENDING

The Quiet Subscriptions

No single subscription feels like real money. Add them up once a year, though, and they read like a bill you never agreed to. Keep the ones you'd re-buy today at full price. Let the rest go.

Applies to you: 5 subscriptions · $94.45 a month · $1,133 a year.

About Ore & Tide

What this is, and what it isn't.

So we built the system — a dashboard that takes your income, bills, debts and goals, and turns them into one clear decision every payday: what goes to bills, what's safe to spend, and what builds your future.
